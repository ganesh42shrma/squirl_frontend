Which banks can it read?

Where is my ledger kept?

In your own database, on the machine where Squirl runs.

What if two statements overlap?

Rows imported before are recognised and skipped.

Can I undo a statement import?

Yes. Undo removes every row that import added.

Can I hide amounts in the ledger?

Guest mode swaps every amount for dots until you turn it off.
